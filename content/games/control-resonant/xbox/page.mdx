## Performance: Digital Foundry's Xbox Verdict

Digital Foundry's console technical review (September 2026) put the Xbox versions through their paces:

| Console | Mode | Resolution | Frame Rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Series X** | Performance | ~864p (FSR upscale) | 60 FPS target — parity with PS5 |
| **Series X** | Quality | ~1260p | 30 FPS, stable |
| **Series S** | Single mode | ~864p (Series X Performance-equivalent settings) | 30 FPS, generally steady |

- **Series X matches the base PS5** on visuals and performance — identical 864p/1260p mode splits, solid performance, with occasional frame drops and minor screen tearing when the frame budget is exceeded.
- **Series S is a one-mode console here:** the ~864p presentation runs at a steady 30 FPS with settings comparable to the Series X Performance mode — but no graphics options.
- **No ray tracing on any Xbox** — hardware RT is exclusive to the PS5 Pro. If image quality is your top priority, see the [PS5 version guide](/games/control-resonant/ps5) for the DF-tested Pro numbers.
- **Series S boss caveat:** the first-boss encounter (heavy transparency effects) causes significant drops on Series S, same as on other base consoles.
