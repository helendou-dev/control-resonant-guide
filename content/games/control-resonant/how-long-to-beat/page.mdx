**Short answer: Control Resonant takes roughly 30 hours to finish the main story and around 50 hours to see everything.** That figure comes straight from lead designer Sergey Mokhov, and it makes the sequel Remedy's biggest game to date — nearly triple the length of 2019's *Control*.

If you're deciding whether to pre-order, planning your launch week, or just wondering if this fits into your backlog before the holiday season, this page breaks the numbers down by playstyle so you know exactly what you're signing up for.

## Playtime at a Glance

| Playstyle | Estimated Time |
|---|---|
| **Main story only** | ~30 hours |
| **Main story + major side content** | ~38–42 hours |
| **100% completion** | ~50 hours |
| **Control (2019) main story** (for comparison) | ~11–13 hours |
| **Control (2019) + Foundation & AWE DLC** | ~20–24 hours |

<ProTip title="The one number to remember">
50 hours for 100% is a *complete* playthrough — every side quest, every Aberrant weapon mastery track, every collectible codex entry, and full map clearance. Most players land between the 30-hour and 42-hour marks.
</ProTip>

## Why Is Control Resonant So Much Longer Than Control?

The original *Control* was a critically loved but tightly scoped game — one building, one character arc, a dozen or so missions. *Control Resonant* scales up in three directions at once:

1. **The setting.** Instead of the Oldest House, you're exploring a warped, Hiss-corrupted Manhattan — Remedy's largest and most open play space ever. Traversal between districts alone adds hours compared to *Control*'s Brutalist interior loops.
2. **The weapon system.** The Aberrant's five forms — [Dual Blades, Axe, Scythe, Whip, and Heavy Hammer](/games/control-resonant/aberrant-weapon-forms) — each come with their own upgrade paths and mastery challenges. Players who want proficiency in every form will spend significantly longer than story-only players.
3. **Side content density.** Remedy has confirmed optional investigations, lost sectors, and paranatural anomalies far beyond anything in the first game. Skipping them is viable; ignoring them entirely is genuinely hard.

## Main Story: ~30 Hours

The 30-hour figure refers to a focused run: critical path missions, minimal detours, boss fights, and the story of Dylan Faden searching for his missing sister Jesse through a collapsing Manhattan.

Factors that push your personal number up or down:

- **Difficulty.** Hard mode adds meaningful time — Remedy's combat encounters punish sloppy play, and some late-game encounters are brutal without [proper build planning](/games/control-resonant/combat-systems-preview).
- **Deaths and retries.** Boss checkpoints are fair, but "Gap" resource management means an underprepared loadout costs you real minutes per wipe.
- **Cutscene watching.** It's a Remedy game — live-action interludes and long-form dialogue are part of the package. Skip-at-your-own-peril.

## Side Content: +8–12 Hours

Between the main story and full completion sits the bulk of the optional game:

- **Side investigations** — multi-stage optional cases with their own self-contained stories
- **Aberrant mastery challenges** — combat trials tied to each of the five weapon forms
- **Lost sectors** — hidden areas with unique enemies and rewards
- **Altered item hunts** — collectible-driven exploration content echoing the first game's Objects of Power

Players who finish "most but not all" of this content typically report 38–42 hours.

## 100% Completion: ~50 Hours

Full completion includes everything above plus:

- Every codex entry and collectible document (Remedy games hide lore everywhere)
- All weapon forms fully mastered
- All districts fully cleared and mapped
- Ending variations and post-credits content

<InfoBox title="Is there a post-game?">
Yes. After the credits roll, Control Resonant opens up its endgame content and New Game+ remains available. Completionists chasing the full 50 hours will spend a meaningful chunk of that time after the story ends.
</InfoBox>

## How Long With New Game+?

New Game+ carries over your Aberrant progress and unlocks tougher enemy variants. A New Game+ story run typically runs faster than your first playthrough — expect **18–22 hours** if you know the encounters, longer if you're cleaning up missed content on the harder setting.

## Can You Rush It? Speedrun Potential

It's too early for settled speedrun routes, but based on the first game — which saw sub-hour story skips within months — expect the any% category to eventually land well under 10 hours. For normal players, deliberately rushing the critical path while skipping dialogue might compress 30 hours to something closer to 22–24, but you'd be fighting the game's pacing the whole way.

## FAQ

### Is 30 hours just the main story?
Yes. The ~30-hour figure from lead designer Sergey Mokhov covers the critical path. Side content and completion push the total toward 50 hours.

### How long is Control Resonant compared to Control (2019)?
Roughly two to three times longer. Control's main story runs 11–13 hours; Control Resonant's runs about 30.

### Does difficulty affect game length?
Yes. Higher difficulties mean more deaths, more retries, and more time spent upgrading your Aberrant forms to survive. Plan for 20–30% more playtime on hard mode.

### How many hours is 100% completion?
Around 50 hours — every side quest, mastery challenge, collectible, and codex entry.

### Is there New Game+?
Yes, New Game+ is confirmed, carrying over Aberrant progress and remixing enemy difficulty.

### Can I finish it in a weekend?
Not realistically. A launch-weekend binge (say, 15–20 hours) gets you roughly halfway through the story. PS5 players using the [48-hour early access](/games/control-resonant/release-date) get a two-day head start, but you'll still need most of a week of evening sessions to see the credits.

**Expect 30 hours for the story, closer to 50 for everything.** Control Resonant is Remedy's longest game by a wide margin — plan your [pre-order](/games/control-resonant/preorder-guide) and your calendar accordingly, and if you're new to the universe, our [beginner guide](/games/control-resonant/beginner-guide) covers everything you need before September 24, 2026.
