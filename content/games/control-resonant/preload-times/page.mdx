## How to Start Your Pre-Load

Pre-load works automatically if you pre-ordered with auto-download enabled. If it hasn't started automatically:

- **Xbox:** Open **My Games & Apps → Queue** — the pre-load should be sitting there. Select it and choose "Download now" if it's paused.
- **Steam (PC):** The game page in your library shows a **"Pre-load"** button instead of "Pre-purchase." If you've already bought it, Steam may have started downloading automatically — check **Library → Downloads**.
- **Epic (PC):** Open the game page in the Epic Games Store — pre-load status shows there, and purchases get an email when it's ready.
- **PS5:** Open the PlayStation Store and confirm your pre-order is in **Library → Purchased**. When the pre-load window opens (Sept 20 for Deluxe, Sept 22 for Standard), the game downloads automatically if auto-download is enabled under **Settings → System → Power Saving → Automatic Downloads**.

## How Pre-Load Actually Works

Pre-load downloads the encrypted game files ahead of launch. What you can and can't do:

- ✅ **Download everything before launch** — launch day is just an unlock, not a 90+ GB wait;
- ✅ **Keep playing other games** — pre-load runs in the background on consoles;
- ❌ **Play before the unlock time** — files stay encrypted until the unlock timestamp (Sept 22 for PS5 Deluxe, Sept 24 for everyone else). No launch-day workaround, and don't trust any "cracked pre-load" claims you see online — they're scams.
