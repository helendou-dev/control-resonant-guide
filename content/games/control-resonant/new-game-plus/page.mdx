*Last updated: September 26, 2026 — covers the retail build, the day-one patch, and Remedy's official post-launch roadmap announced September 25.*

**Short answer: yes, Control Resonant launched with New Game+, and you can start a run the moment the credits roll. Your Aberrant forms, Resonance abilities, and map/collectible progress all carry over — story progress doesn't. And the day after launch, Remedy confirmed the thing NG+ players wanted most: an NG++ mode in October that lets you chain run after run without wiping progress.**

Here's the complete picture, including the one story choice that's locked per playthrough — the real reason most players will want a second cycle.

## NG+ at Launch: Confirmed and Immediate

Unlike a lot of modern action games that bolt NG+ on weeks or months later, Control Resonant shipped with it on day one:

- **Available immediately after beating the game** — no waiting, no unlock quest.
- **Built as a power-fantasy loop**, not a "continue your save" mode: you replay the campaign from the start, but as a fully-leveled Dylan.
- **Tuned for maxed builds** — NG+ carries a higher difficulty curve aimed at characters that already have everything.

## What Carries Over into NG+

| Carries over | What it means in practice |
|---|---|
| **Aberrant form mastery** | You start NG+ with your full five-form weapon kit, upgrades included — no re-earning forms through the story |
| **Resonance abilities** | Every paranatural power you unlocked stays unlocked |
| **Explored map & collectibles** | Files and upgrades you already found stay marked as discovered |
| **Exclusive NG+ rewards** | Repeat cycles add exclusive form skins and completionist goals |

## What Resets

- **Story progression** — the campaign restarts from the beginning, cutscenes and all.
- **Quest-gated unlocks** — anything the story hands out gets re-gated behind the missions that grant it.
- **Key items and door unlocks** — standard Remedy post-game design: the world resets around your over-leveled character.

NG+ is not a difficulty select, and it's not a save extender. It's the designed endgame: the place where [the build system](/games/control-resonant/skill-tree) and [the Gap combat loop](/games/control-resonant/combat-systems-preview) finally get to run at full power against content tuned for it.

## The Real Reason to Plan an NG+ Run: The Mold Choice

This is the one place Control Resonant asks you to commit permanently — no respec escape hatch:

> After the first Resonant boss, Dylan chooses between **channeling Mold through his arm** or **summoning an autonomous Mold tentacle**. The choice locks for the entire playthrough.

**Only New Game+ lets you combine both.** Powers carry over between cycles, so a second run is the only way to experience the other half of the Mold kit — or run a build that uses the two together. If you're mid-first-playthrough reading this: don't stress the choice, but know that whatever you skip this run is waiting for you in NG+.

And respec within a run stays free: you can rebuild at any safe zone (yes, even mid-NG+), and the [day-one patch](/games/control-resonant/review-embargo) made respeccing cheaper — vendors across the game stock more Reset Items, and The Artist boss now drops two instead of one.

## NG++ Is Coming in October — Here's What It Does

The day after launch, Remedy published its post-launch roadmap. The headline for replay-value players: **New Game Plus Plus arrives in October 2026.**

The problem NG++ solves is the one every NG+ eventually hits: you want to run the game again, but starting a new NG+ means erasing the run you just finished. NG++ removes that — in Remedy's own words, you'll be able to *"start NG+ run after NG+ run, without needing to erase all your progress. Do 665 runs if you want!"*

What this means practically:

- **Your first NG+ becomes a permanent save tier** — progress you earned there stops being at risk.
- **Endless cycling** — NG++ opens the door to challenge runs, build variety runs, and [trophy clean-up](/games/control-resonant/everything-we-know-so-far) (all 50 trophies unlock on any difficulty, Assist Mode included) without starting from zero.
- **Exact October date: TBA.** Remedy has not pinned a day yet — watch their official channels, or this page, for the confirmed date.

### The Rest of the Official Roadmap

| Update | Window | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **New Game Plus Plus (NG++)** | October 2026 | ✅ Officially announced |
| **Photo Mode** | November 2026 | ✅ Officially announced |

Photo Mode's November window comes with a candid explanation from Remedy for why it missed launch: *"we needed to prioritize making sure the game was in the best possible shape."* The studio says it consulted community virtual photographers while building the mode — a good sign it's being treated as a real feature, not a checkbox.

## Is NG+ Worth It? The Honest Math

- Main story: **~30 hours**. Full completion: **~50 hours** ([length breakdown](/games/control-resonant/how-long-to-beat)).
- A full NG+ cycle on top lands you at **60–100 hours total**, with the Aberrant's full kit available from the opening chapter.
- The higher NG+ difficulty curve is tuned for maxed builds — this is where the [Aberrant's five forms](/games/control-resonant/aberrant-weapon-forms) get to show what they can do without progression gates.
- Completionists get exclusive form skins; build-tinkerers get the only legitimate way to combine both Mold powers.

If you liked the first *Control*'s post-game loop, this is a straight upgrade. If you found the base campaign enough, nothing here is missable — but the NG++ announcement means the door stays open indefinitely, so there's no rush.

## FAQ

### Does Control Resonant have New Game+?
Yes — it launched with the game on September 24, 2026, and you can start a run immediately after finishing the campaign. Aberrant form mastery, Resonance abilities, and map/collectible progress carry over; story progression and quest-gated unlocks reset.

### What is NG++ and when does it arrive?
New Game Plus Plus, officially announced September 25, 2026 for **October 2026**. It lets you start NG+ runs back-to-back without erasing previous NG+ progress — Remedy's pitch: "Do 665 runs if you want!" The exact date within October is not yet announced.

### Does the Mold power choice carry into NG+?
Yes. Because Resonance abilities persist between cycles, NG+ is the only way to combine the arm-channel and tentacle Mold powers — the one choice in the game that's permanent per playthrough.

### Can I respec inside NG+?
Yes. Respec remains free at safe zones during NG+ runs, and the day-one patch increased Reset Item stock at vendors (The Artist boss now drops two) so rebuilds are cheaper than at review time.

### Do NG+ or NG++ affect trophies?
No. All 50 trophies (1 Platinum, 1 Gold, 16 Silver, 32 Bronze) unlock on any difficulty with Assist Mode available throughout — there are no difficulty-locked or NG+-gated trophies.

### Does photo mode have a date?
Not beyond "November 2026." Remedy says it consulted community virtual photographers and prioritized launch-window polish over shipping the mode day one.

### Is NG+ harder?
Yes — NG+ runs a higher difficulty curve tuned for fully-leveled characters. If you want the reverse, [Assist Mode's 11 settings](/games/control-resonant/difficulty-settings) work in NG+ too, in either direction.
