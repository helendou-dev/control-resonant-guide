## Trailer #1 — TGA 2025 Announcement Trailer (December 11, 2025)

**Where it debuted:** The Game Awards 2025 (Los Angeles), December 11, 2025.

**What it showed:** The title reveal, plus the first gameplay footage of Dylan Faden — Remedy's first new mainline protagonist since 2019's *Control*. The trailer opens with a live-action staged moment (a performer suspended above an onstage cage that echoes Dylan's containment cell from the first game), then cuts to in-engine cinematics and a brief slice of melee combat with the shape-shifting Aberrant. By the end, Jesse Faden appears and addresses the camera — establishing the core narrative hook for the sequel: Dylan as the playable lead, Jesse as the missing-in-action Director the player is searching for.

**Key beats:**
- First-ever title reveal of "Control Resonant" (previously known only by its Heron codename).
- First gameplay glimpse of Dylan Faden in combat with the Aberrant.
- First confirmation that Jesse is alive but has "gone rogue" — a major narrative reframe from her canonical ending in *Control*.
- Platform and release window confirmed: 2026 on PS5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC.

**Why it matters:** This is the trailer that turns four years of speculation about "Control 2" into a real product. The Jesse-rogue framing also rewrites sequel expectations — the search-for-sister hook is positioned as the spine of the entire narrative, not a subplot.

For the full narrative setup this trailer established, see our [Control story recap](/games/control-resonant/story-recap).

## Trailer #2 — State of Play Gameplay Trailer (February 12, 2026)

**Where it debuted:** PlayStation State of Play, February 12, 2026.

**What it showed:** Remedy's first dedicated gameplay trailer — roughly 2 minutes of in-engine footage showing Dylan exploring a warped Manhattan, the Aberrant shifting forms mid-combo, and a first look at the game's vertical combat identity. No release date yet, but the trailer confirmed the action-RPG genre pivot (melee-heavy, not a third-person shooter like the original *Control*).

**Key beats:**
- First extended look at the **Aberrant** in action — the shape-shifting melee weapon that replaces the Service Weapon.
- First clear visuals of Manhattan's "warped" zones, with gravity-bending architecture hinting at the Gravity Anomaly design later confirmed in hands-on previews.
- Confirmed the genre shift: action-RPG with melee focus, not a shooter.
- Platform confirmation narrowed to current-gen only (PS5, Xbox Series X|S, PC) — no last-gen versions.

**Why it matters:** This is the trailer that ended the "will it be a shooter like Control 1?" debate. Once fans saw Dylan in melee with a transforming weapon, the action-RPG identity was locked in.

## Trailer #3 — State of Play Story Trailer + Release Date Reveal (June 2, 2026)

**Where it debuted:** PlayStation State of Play, June 2, 2026.

**What it showed:** The first true Story Trailer — a roughly 2-minute cinematic showing Dylan escaping captivity, walking out into a fractured Manhattan, and reuniting with Jesse Faden. The trailer ends on a tense face-to-face between the siblings. Most importantly, it locked in the worldwide release date: **September 24, 2026** for PS5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC, with the Mac version following later in 2026.

**Key beats:**
- **Release date confirmed:** September 24, 2026 worldwide, digital-first.
- **First clear Dylan–Jesse reunion** — the trailer's emotional centerpiece, showing both siblings alive and aware of each other for the first time on-screen.
- Mikael Kasurinen's PlayStation Blog post confirmed Dylan is the **sole playable character** and Jesse "shapes the path" from outside.
- Same-day confirmation that pre-orders are live across all platforms, with the Hiss Corruption Outfit + Pickpocket's Tool Artifact as universal bonuses and the Occult Outfit as a PS5-exclusive pre-order extra.
- The story trailer version released to the public on **June 5** featured an original song: *"Manalan mailla"* ("In the Lands of the Underworld") by Finnish ethno-pop artist Vilma Jää — the only original song released as part of the marketing campaign.

**Why it matters:** This is the trailer that turned Control Resonant from "coming 2026" into "coming September 24." Every subsequent trailer since has been building on the audience this one captured. The Vilma Jää song also positioned the soundtrack as a marketing asset in its own right.

For everything you can do once the date is locked, see our [pre-order guide](/games/control-resonant/preorder-guide) and [release date guide](/games/control-resonant/release-date).

## Trailer #4 — Summer Game Fest "Paranatural Manhattan" Trailer (June 5, 2026)

**Where it debuted:** Summer Game Fest 2026 main stage, June 5, 2026.

**What it showed:** A world-focused trailer narrated by **Emily Pope** — Jesse's former FBC colleague, returning in *Resonant* as acting co-director of the Bureau. The trailer is short on combat and long on environments: multiple distinct Manhattan zones (surface streets, underground areas, and otherworldly dimensions), the promise of new Oldest House areas never before explored, and the dynamic boss / Altered Item / puzzle triangle that defines each zone's content loop. The original song "Manalan mailla" returns as the soundtrack.

**Key beats:**
- **First proper look at Manhattan's zone design** — multiple distinct biomes with their own enemy factions and visual identities, confirming the "open-ended zones" approach Remedy has described since the 2025 announcement.
- **First hint of new Oldest House areas** beyond what was explorable in *Control (2019)*.
- **Emily Pope confirmed as acting FBC Director** — the first canonical narrative confirmation of who is running the Bureau during Jesse's absence.
- **Pre-orders officially opened** during the SGF window, with prices revealed: Standard $59.99 / Digital Deluxe $69.99.
- Same-day announcement of a limited-edition **iam8bit merchandise collaboration** (FBC mug, T-shirt, agent cap, hoodie).

**Why it matters:** Three days after the Story Trailer announced the date, this trailer showed what players would actually be doing in that time. The zone-focused framing also confirmed the "not a seamless open world, but large distinct districts" design choice Remedy has held to through every hands-on preview since.

## Trailer #5 — PC Gaming Show "Take CONTROL" Trailer + Developer Mini-Documentary (June 8, 2026)

**Where it debuted:** PC Gaming Show 2026, June 8, 2026.

**What it showed:** A short, gameplay-focused trailer (under 2 minutes) showing Dylan in active combat, plus a **separate exclusive mini-documentary** featuring creative director **Mikael Kasurinen**, art director **Elmeri Raitanen**, and lead level designer **Anne-Marie Grönroos** walking through how Manhattan's paranatural zones are designed, how gravity-defying traversal works, and how melee + abilities + reactive environments combine into the "dynamic action-RPG sandbox" the developers are targeting. Released on PC Gamer's YouTube channel the same day.

**Key beats:**
- **First real combat-focused trailer** — Dylan's full kit on display: form swaps, abilities, aerial combat, and the gap-stagger-finisher loop that defines the new combat system.
- First developer-facing content (the mini-doc) showing the **art direction and level design philosophy** behind Manhattan's zones, narrated by the actual designers.
- Confirmed full launch support for NVIDIA RTX tech: **DLSS 4.5**, path tracing, RTX Mega Geometry, Ray Reconstruction, and Dynamic Multi Frame Generation.
- Confirmed the game's **localization scope** — the most-localized Remedy title to date, with full audio + interface + subtitles in 8 languages (English, Brazilian Portuguese, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, Spanish-Spain) and subtitle/interface support in 7 more (Korean, Polish, Russian, Spanish-LATAM, Traditional Chinese, Turkish, Ukrainian).

**Why it matters:** This is the trailer (and mini-doc) that turned PC players from "interested" to "checking system requirements." The localization confirmation also opened the door to non-English-speaking markets that previous Remedy titles had partially shut out.

For the hardware side of those PC requirements, see our [system requirements guide](/games/control-resonant/system-requirements).

## Trailer #6 — ChinaJoy 2026 Trailer (July 30 – August 2, 2026)

**Where it debuted:** Remedy's booth at ChinaJoy 2026 (Shanghai), July 30 – August 2, 2026. Posted publicly to Remedy's official **bilibili** channel during the show.

**What it showed:** A region-targeted trailer introducing several firsts for the Asian market, plus a striking opening scene that hasn't appeared in any English-language trailer: **Jesse Faden driving the Aberrant into Dylan's chest** while he lies comatose, followed by an older Dylan awakening. The trailer also features the **first Chinese-language dub** of any Control Resonant footage, narrated in Mandarin. Senior Community Manager **Vida Starčević** presented the trailer on stage, accompanied by a reveal of a **region-exclusive Dylan outfit** — *"The Robe of the Expert from the Ministry of Immaterial Objectives"* — designed specifically for Chinese players, with in-lore description referencing a fictional Chinese agency dedicated to containing supernatural phenomena.

**Key beats:**
- **First Chinese-dubbed trailer** in the marketing cycle — Remedy's most-localized game ever, demonstrated on stage.
- **First visual confirmation of the game's opening**: Jesse stabs the Aberrant into Dylan (while he's comatose), and Dylan later awakens "older" — confirming the game's narrative begins with Dylan's awakening after years of containment.
- **Region-exclusive Dylan outfit** revealed: the Robe of the Expert from the Ministry of Immaterial Objectives, a fictional Chinese bureau equivalent to the FBC. (This outfit was not included with global pre-orders.)
- Playable demo at the Remedy booth — the **first Chinese-market hands-on** event for the game.

**Why it matters:** The ChinaJoy trailer confirmed two things every Western fan was guessing about: the game's literal opening (Dylan awakens after years in containment) and the relationship framing between the siblings (Jesse is not absent — she's an active, present figure who may be shaping Dylan's reality from the start). The region-exclusive outfit also set expectations for how Remedy will handle post-launch content for non-Western markets.

## Trailer #7 — gamescom "Altered State" Trailer (August 25, 2026)

**Where it debuted:** gamescom Opening Night Live, Cologne, August 25, 2026.

**What it showed:** The marketing cycle's final pre-launch trailer — and the most action-heavy one yet. Cut to The Prodigy's **"Omen"** (the first time a licensed Western song has been used in the campaign), the trailer shows Dylan tearing through warped Manhattan at full speed: full aerial combat, Aberrant form swaps mid-combo, executions, and what the developers call "unpredictable encounters." Alongside the trailer, Remedy also announced a real-world **fashion collaboration with ZA/UM Atelier** — a five-piece made-to-order apparel line inspired by FBC wardrobe, displayed at the gamescom booth and available for pre-order through ZA/UM.

**Key beats:**
- **First public hands-on demo** at gamescom (Level Infinite booth, Hall 6, August 26–30) — the first time anyone outside press previews could play the game.
- The Prodigy's "Omen" — the loudest, fastest song used in the entire campaign, signaling that this is the action showcase.
- **Digital September 24 launch reaffirmed** with no date changes — the game was already gone gold (August 18), so the trailer confirmed what was already true.
- **ZA/UM Atelier fashion collaboration** — Remedy's first major real-world apparel tie-in.
- A final teaser for a **PS5 demo at Tokyo Game Show** (Sept 17–21), the last public hands-on before launch.

**Why it matters:** This is the trailer that ended the marketing campaign's "announce" half and started the "ship it" half. It's also the only trailer to pair with a public playable demo, making it the first time fans could verify the trailer's claims with their own hands.

## The Marketing Arc, in 30 Seconds

Remedy's trailer strategy for Control Resonant followed a clean three-act structure:

1. **December 2025 – February 2026 — "What is it?"** The TGA reveal and first gameplay trailer established Dylan, the Aberrant, and Manhattan's warped setting.
2. **June 2026 — "When and how much?"** A four-trailer burst (State of Play Story + SGF + PC Gaming Show, all in one week) locked in the release date, pricing, gameplay systems, and localization scope — basically the full commercial pitch in a single week.
3. **Late July – August 2026 — "Try it."** ChinaJoy and gamescom trailers paired the final marketing push with the first public hands-on opportunities — and teased the final TGS demo before launch.

If a launch trailer ships in the next two weeks, it'll be the closer: a single, polished push to convert the 1.5M+ wishlists Remedy announced in August into day-one players.

Seven official trailers as of September 9, 2026 — from the TGA 2025 title reveal on December 11, 2025 to the "Altered State" trailer on August 25, 2026.
