## How to Start Your Preload

Preload works automatically if you pre-ordered with auto-download enabled — check your queue for a pending download. If it hasn't started automatically:

- **Xbox:** Open **My Games & Apps → Queue** — the preload should be sitting there. Select it and choose "Download now" if it's paused.
- **Steam (PC):** The game page in your library will show a **"Pre-load"** button instead of "Pre-purchase." If you've already bought it, Steam may have started downloading automatically — check **Library → Downloads**.
- **Epic (PC):** Open the game page in the Epic Games Store — preload status shows there, and purchases get an email when it's ready.
- **PS5:** Not live yet. When it opens, the store page shows a countdown, and enabled **Settings → Storage → auto-download** handles it.

## How Preload Actually Works

Preload downloads the encrypted game files ahead of launch. What you can and can't do:

- ✅ **Download everything before launch** — launch day is just an unlock, not a 100 GB wait;
- ✅ **Keep playing other games** — preload runs in the background on consoles;
- ❌ **Play before the unlock time** — files stay encrypted until September 24 (or September 22 on PS5 early access). No launch-day workaround, and don't trust any "cracked preload" claims you see online — they're scams.
