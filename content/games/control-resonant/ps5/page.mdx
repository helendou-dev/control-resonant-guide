## Performance: What Digital Foundry Actually Measured

**Update (September 22, 2026):** Digital Foundry has published its full console technical review of Control Resonant, and the headline for PS5 buyers is simple: **the game runs, the image quality is the compromise.**

### PS5 (base console)

| Mode | Internal Resolution | Output | Frame Rate | Ray Tracing |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Performance** | ~864p (FSR upscale) | ~1440p | 60 FPS target — mostly held | ❌ |
| **Quality** | ~1260p | Near-4K | 30 FPS, stable | ❌ |

- **Performance mode generally holds 60**, with drops largely confined to traversal stutters and heavy transparency effects during one specific boss encounter (the first).
- **Quality mode is the sharper image** at a stable 30 FPS, with modestly improved shadow coherency and reflection clarity.
- **Neither mode has hardware ray tracing** — that's PS5 Pro only.
- **Image quality is the weak spot on base PS5:** Digital Foundry found "substantial breakup and a poor resolve on fine details," with cascaded shadow maps that look incoherent at distance and screen-space reflections that lose detail when on-screen content is occluded. The combat camera, which follows Dylan at a distance to frame brawls, can also collide awkwardly with walls and geometry.
- The lighting itself holds up: rich diffuse global illumination, volumetric lighting, atmospheric fog, and the physics-driven destruction the series is known for.

### PS5 Pro — the only console with ray tracing

| Mode | Internal Resolution | Output | Frame Rate | Ray Tracing |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Performance** | ~864p (PSSR upscale) | 4K | 60 FPS, smoother than base | ❌ |
| **Quality** | ~1224p | 4K | 30 FPS | ✅ RT reflections + shadows |
| **Balanced** | ~1080p | 4K | 40 FPS (VRR displays) | ✅ RT reflections + shadows |

- **PSSR is a genuine step up:** reconstructing the 864p internal image to a 4K target significantly mitigates the shimmering and aliasing artifacts seen on base hardware.
- **Hardware ray tracing is Pro-exclusive** — just like *Alan Wake 2*. Quality mode adds RT reflections across glossy city surfaces and RT shadows with variable-penumbra contact detail; Balanced keeps the same RT feature set at 1080p targeting 40 FPS for VRR displays (frame rate is less consistent there).
- Digital Foundry's verdict: the PS5 Pro provides "a noticeable step up in overall visual quality and stability" and is the most complete console presentation.

### The honest summary

- **PS5 base:** playable and smooth in Performance mode, but expect visible image-quality compromises — shimmering, soft fine detail, distant shadow artifacts.
- **PS5 Pro:** the console version to have if image quality matters to you — PSSR plus the only console ray tracing.
- **Day-one patches are inevitable** — Remedy shipped the launch build before the physical delay, and DF's review flags specific fixes (shadow cascades, SSR, camera occlusion) that post-launch patches could address. Digital Foundry also notes capable PCs with RT hardware and ML upscaling offer a stronger overall experience than any console.

## FAQ

### When can I play Control Resonant on PS5?
September 22, 2026 — if you pre-order the Digital Deluxe Edition, thanks to the 48-hour early access. Otherwise September 24.

### Is the PS5 early access on Standard too?
No — the 48-hour early access is exclusive to the PS5 Digital Deluxe Edition ($69.99). The Standard edition ($59.99) unlocks September 24. On PS5, the Deluxe's extra $10 buys early access plus the AWE Mission Outfit, Untapped Artifact (Wallet), Starter Resource Bundle, digital artbook, and soundtrack.

### Does Control Resonant use the DualSense adaptive triggers?
Yes — confirmed. The Aberrant's five weapon forms reportedly map to distinct trigger resistance, and haptic feedback is confirmed for combat hits and the Gap stagger system.

### Will Control Resonant run at 60 FPS on PS5?
**Yes — Digital Foundry-tested.** Performance mode targets 60 FPS and generally holds it (~864p internal, FSR-upscaled to ~1440p), with drops mostly limited to traversal stutters and one transparency-heavy first-boss encounter. Quality mode is the alternative: ~1260p near-4K at a stable 30 FPS.

### Is there a PS5 Pro enhanced version?
**Yes — and it's the best console version.** Digital Foundry tested it: PSSR reconstructs the ~864p image to 4K (removing most of the base console's shimmer), and the Pro is the **only console with hardware ray tracing** — RT reflections and shadows in Quality (1224p/30 FPS) and Balanced (1080p/40 FPS for VRR displays) modes, just like *Alan Wake 2*.

### Does the base PS5 version have ray tracing?
No. Hardware ray tracing is exclusive to PS5 Pro on consoles. Base PS5 and Xbox Series X|S offer Quality/Performance modes without RT.

### How much storage does Control Resonant need on PS5?
**89.27 GB confirmed**, per PlayStation Game Size (September 8). Plus day-one patches — budget ~100 GB on your SSD to be safe. The pre-load opens September 20 (Deluxe) / September 22 (Standard).

### Is Control Resonant coming to PS4?
No. It's a current-gen and PC title — no PS4 version exists or is planned.

### Will Control Resonant be on PlayStation Plus / Game Pass?
No PS Plus or Game Pass release has been announced.
