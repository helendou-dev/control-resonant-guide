**Short answer: Control Resonant is NOT on Game Pass — at least not as a day-one title.** As of August 26, 2026, Remedy has not announced any Game Pass deal, day-one or otherwise. The game launches September 24 on PS5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC (Steam + Epic) at full retail price, with no subscription workaround on Xbox.

That's the whole truth in one line — but if you're an Xbox owner hoping for a Game Pass surprise, here's the full picture, including why it's *unlikely* to change before launch.

## What's Confirmed

Remedy self-published Control Resonant (it bought the IP back from 505 Games in February 2024) with Annapurna co-financing. In the run-up to launch, the studio has confirmed:

- **Launch platforms:** PS5, Xbox Series X|S, PC (Steam + Epic), GeForce NOW, and Mac (later in 2026).
- **No Game Pass deal has been signed or announced** — no day-one listing, no "coming to Game Pass" tweet, nothing.
- **Pre-orders are open everywhere**, and the game has passed 1.5 million wishlists across platforms.

If a Game Pass announcement were coming, it would typically land *before* pre-orders hit full swing — publishers announce subscription deals weeks ahead of launch to convert fence-sitters. We're four weeks out, and there's been radio silence.

## Why Everyone Assumes It'll Be on Game Pass

The confusion is fair, because Remedy's history is genuinely mixed:

| Remedy title | Game Pass history |
|---|---|
| **Control (2019)** | Added to Game Pass in 2023 (years after launch) |
| **Alan Wake Remastered** | Added to Game Pass in 2023 |
| **Alan Wake 2** | **Never on Game Pass** — digital-only, full price |
| **Control Resonant** | No announcement — but Alan Wake 2's precedent is the stronger signal |

The key pattern: **Remedy's older catalogue has gone to Game Pass, but its *new* releases haven't.** Alan Wake 2 launched in 2023 without Game Pass and still hasn't appeared there. That's the closest model for what to expect from Control Resonant.

## When Would We Actually Know?

There are two realistic scenarios:

1. **A deal gets announced before September 24** — unlikely at this stage, but not impossible. Microsoft has surprised with late-day-one announcements before.
2. **Control Resonant joins Game Pass months or years after launch** — the most likely path, matching both Control (2023) and Alan Wake 2's (non) history. If you're patient, wait for a sale instead.

## What Xbox Players Should Do

If you're an Xbox owner and Game Pass is your normal way to play:

- **Don't wait on a subscription miracle for launch day.** If you want to play September 24, buy it — the [Standard edition at $59.99](/games/control-resonant/preorder-guide) is the move.
- **No early access on Xbox anyway** — the 48-hour early window is PS5 Digital Deluxe only, so there's zero penalty to waiting for day-one reviews before you commit.
- **If you're a patient gamer**, the safest play is to skip the launch and wait for either a post-launch discount or a future Game Pass drop. Neither is guaranteed, but both are historically likely within 12 months.

## FAQ

### Is Control Resonant coming to Game Pass on day one?
No. No day-one Game Pass deal has been announced, and with the game four weeks from launch, it would be surprising (though not impossible) for one to appear now.

### Will Control Resonant be on Game Pass later?
Possibly. Remedy's older titles (Control, Alan Wake Remastered) are on Game Pass, but its recent releases are not — Alan Wake 2 has never appeared there. Expect a wait of months, not days, if it ever arrives.

### Is Control Resonant on PS Plus Extra / Premium?
No announcement has been made for PlayStation Plus either. Same situation as Game Pass: no subscription deal confirmed.

### Can I play Control Resonant without buying it?
Not legally. There's no demo, no beta, and no free tier — see our [demo / beta status page](/games/control-resonant/demo-beta-playtest) for details. Your options are buying it or streaming it on GeForce NOW if you own a copy.

### What's the cheapest way to play Control Resonant on Xbox?
The Standard edition at $59.99. The Deluxe's $10 upgrade is cosmetics + digital extras only, and Xbox doesn't get early access — so there's no gameplay reason to spend the extra $10 unless you want the skins. Full breakdown: [Standard vs Deluxe](/games/control-resonant/preorder-guide).

### I own Game Pass — does that give me a discount on Control Resonant?
No. Game Pass members get a 10% discount on *some* Xbox store purchases, and Microsoft has applied it to many full-price titles, but it's not guaranteed for every game. The day-one purchase price is $59.99 for Game Pass subscribers like everyone else.
