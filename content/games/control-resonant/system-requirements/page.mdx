Control Resonant launches September 24, 2026 on PC via **Steam and the Epic Games Store** — and Remedy has now published the **final, official PC system requirements** (announced September 9, with just over two weeks to launch).

The headline: the low end is friendlier than most AAA launches in 2026 — a **GTX 1070** gets you through the door at 1080p/30 — but the ceiling is brutal: full **path tracing at 4K/60 demands an RTX 5080**. And every single tier requires **16 GB of RAM and a 120 GB SSD**. Here's the complete breakdown.

<InfoBox title="Status: official, straight from Remedy">
These are Remedy's final published specs for the Northlight-engine PC build, announced September 9, 2026 — six tiers covering standard play, ray tracing, and path tracing. No more estimates on this page; everything below is confirmed.

## Official System Requirements: All Six Tiers

Remedy's spec sheet is built around six performance targets. Note that **every tier assumes an upscaler is active** (Quality at 1080p, Balanced at 1440p, Performance at 4K) — this is a game designed around DLSS/FSR from the ground up, not one that merely tolerates them.

### Minimum (1080p / 30 FPS)

| Component | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **CPU** | Intel Core i5-8500 / AMD Ryzen 5 3500 |
| **GPU** | NVIDIA GTX 1070 / AMD RX 5600 XT / Intel Arc A580 |
| **VRAM** | 6 GB |
| **RAM** | 16 GB |
| **OS** | Windows 10/11 64-bit |
| **Storage** | 120 GB SSD |
| **Settings** | Upscaler: Quality · Graphics: Low · Ray Tracing: Off |

### Recommended (1440p / 60 FPS)

| Component | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **CPU** | Intel Core i7-10700 / AMD Ryzen 7 3700X |
| **GPU** | NVIDIA RTX 3060 Ti / AMD RX 6700 XT / Intel Arc B580 |
| **VRAM** | 8 GB |
| **Settings** | Upscaler: Balanced · Graphics: Medium · Ray Tracing: Off |

### Enthusiast (4K / 60 FPS)

| Component | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **CPU** | Intel Core i7-10700 / AMD Ryzen 7 3700X |
| **GPU** | NVIDIA RTX 3080 / AMD RX 6800 XT |
| **VRAM** | 10 GB |
| **Settings** | Upscaler: Performance · Graphics: High · Ray Tracing: Off |

### Ray Tracing Minimum (1080p / 30 FPS)

| Component | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **CPU** | Intel Core i7-8700K / AMD Ryzen 5 3600X |
| **GPU** | NVIDIA RTX 2060 SUPER / AMD RX 6600 / Intel Arc A580 |
| **VRAM** | 8 GB |
| **Settings** | Upscaler: Quality · Graphics: Low · Ray Tracing: Low |

### Ray Tracing Recommended (1440p / 60 FPS)

| Component | Requirement |
|---|---|

| **GPU** | NVIDIA RTX 4070 Ti / AMD RX 9070 |
| **VRAM** | 10 GB |
| **Settings** | Upscaler: Balanced · Graphics: Medium · Ray Tracing: Medium |

### Path Tracing Enthusiast (4K / 60 FPS)

| Component | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **CPU** | Intel Core i7-10700 / AMD Ryzen 7 3700X |
| **GPU** | NVIDIA RTX 5080 |
| **VRAM** | 12 GB |
| **Settings** | Upscaler: Performance · Graphics: High · Ray Tracing: High (full path tracing) |

<ProTip title="Read the CPU line closely">
The i7-10700 / Ryzen 7 3700X is the CPU requirement for *four of the six tiers* — Recommended, Enthusiast, RT Recommended, and Path Tracing Enthusiast. In other words, once you're past the minimum, **your GPU is the entire performance conversation**. If you have a modern 6-core+ CPU, spend every upgrade dollar on the graphics card.
</ProTip>

## Confirmed PC Features

Beyond the raw spec tiers, Remedy has confirmed the following for the PC build:

- **NVIDIA DLSS 4.5 and AMD FSR 4.1** upscaling, **with frame generation** — though the official performance targets above were benchmarked *without* frame generation, so those figures are conservative
- **Uncapped framerate**
- **HDR support**
- **Ultrawide monitor support**
- Full **ray tracing and path tracing** support (path tracing at the highest preset)

## What the Specs Actually Tell You

### The low end is genuinely reasonable

A GTX 1070 is a **2016 graphics card**, and it clears the minimum spec for 1080p/30. That's a decade of GPU history eligible for the door — a rarity in 2026's AAA landscape, where current-gen-only releases routinely demand RTX 30-series minimums. Remedy clearly optimized the rasterization path hard. If your machine ran *Alan Wake 2* at your target resolution, you're comfortably in.

### Path tracing is the RTX 5080 tier

The top of the spec sheet is reserved for full **path tracing at 4K/60** — the same bleeding-edge lighting tech that made *Alan Wake 2* the definitive GPU stress test. The RTX 5080 requirement (with 12 GB VRAM and Performance upscaling, no less) confirms Control Resonant is positioned as the next showcase for high-end NVIDIA hardware. If you're not chasing path tracing, though, a **4070 Ti handles 1440p/60 with medium ray tracing** — a much saner target.

### 120 GB of SSD space — triple the original

The original *Control* demanded roughly 40 GB. Resonant asks for **120 GB of SSD**, and while Remedy technically allows HDD installs, expect what PC Gamer politely calls a "sub-optimal experience." Three-digit install sizes are the new normal for current-gen-only open-ended games; budget the space now, especially with a [September 24 launch](/games/control-resonant/release-date) and day-one patches on top.

### Every tier assumes upscaling

This is the quiet paradigm shift in the spec sheet: there is no "native resolution" tier anywhere. Quality at 1080p, Balanced at 1440p, Performance at 4K — Remedy is telling you upscaling is the intended way to play, on every card, at every tier. Factor that into any hardware decisions.

## Control Resonant on Steam Deck?

Not officially confirmed — but the minimum spec is a promising signal. A GTX 1070-class floor with upscaling baked into every target means the Deck's APU (roughly GTX 1050-1060 territory) isn't far off the entry bar at 800p/30 with aggressive FSR. The 120 GB install is also a third of a Deck's 512 GB storage. Treat 30 FPS Deck play as *plausible but unverified*; don't count on Verified status at launch.

## Control Resonant on GeForce NOW?

Yes — Remedy confirmed the game **launches with GeForce NOW support from day one** (announced alongside the September 24 release date). If you're below the GTX 1070 floor or want the path-traced look without RTX 5080 money, cloud streaming is the practical answer. Steam/Epic library linking applies as with other GeForce NOW titles; exact tier availability is confirmed by NVIDIA per game, so check the GeForce NOW app ahead of launch.

## FAQ

### What are the official Control Resonant PC requirements?
Announced September 9: the minimum is a **GTX 1070 / RX 5600 XT / Arc A580, i5-8500 / Ryzen 5 3500, 16 GB RAM, and a 120 GB SSD** for 1080p/30. Six tiers run up to an **RTX 5080** for 4K/60 with path tracing. Full tables above.

### Can I run Control Resonant on 8 GB of RAM?
No — 16 GB is a hard requirement across all six tiers. This is a current-gen-only title; 8 GB machines should look at GeForce NOW instead.

### Is an SSD required?
Officially, 120 GB of storage is required and an SSD is the expectation — HDD installs are described as delivering a sub-optimal experience. Treat the SSD as non-negotiable.

### Will Control Resonant support DLSS and FSR?
Both, at the newest versions: **DLSS 4.5 and FSR 4.1**, including frame generation. The official performance targets already assume upscaling is active (Quality at 1080p, Balanced at 1440p, Performance at 4K).

### Does the game have ray tracing?
Yes — ray tracing at Low/Medium/High presets plus **full path tracing** at the top end, which requires an RTX 5080 for 4K/60. Ray tracing entry-level starts at an RTX 2060 SUPER / RX 6600 for 1080p/30.

### Is the game available on Epic Games Store too?
Yes — Control Resonant launches simultaneously on Steam and the Epic Games Store on [September 24, 2026](/games/control-resonant/release-date), standard edition $59.99, Digital Deluxe $69.99.

## The Bottom Line

The official ladder is **GTX 1070 → RTX 3060 Ti → RTX 3080 → (RT) RTX 4070 Ti → (Path Tracing) RTX 5080**, with **16 GB RAM and a 120 GB SSD** as fixed costs across every tier. The entry point is a decade old and genuinely reasonable; the path-tracing ceiling is a hardware showcase. Mid-range owners (3060 Ti-class) hit the intended 1440p/60 experience without ray tracing — and that's the tier Remedy built the game around.
