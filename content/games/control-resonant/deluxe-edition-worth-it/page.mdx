**Short answer: On PS5, yes — the Deluxe's 48-hour early access alone can justify the $10. On Xbox and PC, it depends entirely on whether you want the cosmetics, artbook, and soundtrack, because there's no early access for you.** Here's the honest, item-by-item breakdown.

## The $10 Question

Control Resonant costs **$59.99 (Standard)** or **$69.99 (Digital Deluxe)**. The $10 difference buys:

| Deluxe content | Value on PS5 | Value on Xbox/PC |
|---|---|---|
| **48-hour early access** (Sept 22) | ⭐ High — only way to play early | ❌ Not included |
| **AWE Mission Outfit** | Cosmetic | Cosmetic |
| **Untapped Artifact (Wallet)** | Small in-game perk | Small in-game perk |
| **Starter Resource Bundle** | Small early boost | Small early boost |
| **Digital artbook** | Fan value | Fan value |
| **Original soundtrack** | Fan value | Fan value |

The game itself is **identical** — same story, same combat, same content. The Deluxe is purely a bundle of perks around the base game, and the single item that changes the math is the PS5 early access.

## The PS5 Case: Where Deluxe Clearly Wins

If you're buying on **PlayStation 5**, the Deluxe is the only edition that grants the **48-hour early access** — you play on **September 22** instead of the 24th. The PS5 Standard edition does *not* include early access.

Why that $10 is easy to justify on PS5:

- **Two extra days of play before the guides flood the internet.** For a single-player Remedy title with a weekend launch, that's the difference between discovering things yourself and having them spoiled.
- **A head start on launch-weekend momentum.** If you're the type who posts screenshots, finishes first, or just wants bragging rights, two days is real.
- **Pre-load timing.** PS5 pre-loads are expected ~Sept 19–20, so a Deluxe pre-order means everything is downloaded and ready to unlock at the earliest possible moment.
- **The extras are genuinely decent** — the AWE Mission Outfit is one of the better alternate outfits Remedy has shipped, and the soundtrack is a proper ~40-track release, not a 10-song sampler.

On PS5, the Deluxe is effectively the "play the game properly on launch weekend" edition. If you know you're buying Control Resonant on PlayStation anyway, the $10 is the cheapest early-access upgrade Remedy has ever offered.

## The Xbox / PC Case: No Early Access, So It's All About Extras

On **Xbox Series X|S and PC (Steam/Epic)**, the Deluxe includes the same content list but **no early access** — everyone unlocks September 24, Standard or Deluxe. So the $10 buys:

- AWE Mission Outfit (cosmetic)
- Untapped Artifact (Wallet) + Starter Resource Bundle (small in-game perks)
- Digital artbook + soundtrack (fan content)

**Worth it if:** you're a Remedy completionist, you always buy deluxe editions, or you listen to game soundtracks. The artbook for a game like this is genuinely collectible-quality.

**Not worth it if:** you just want to play the game. The in-game perks are small, the outfit is cosmetic, and there's no early access on your platform. Nothing in the Deluxe is missable forever — cosmetic packs and artbooks from Remedy's past titles have all been sold separately or bundled in later sales.

## What You're NOT Getting (Be Clear About This)

- **No extra story content.** The Deluxe adds zero missions, areas, or gameplay features.
- **No early access on Xbox or PC.** The 48-hour window is a PS5-exclusive perk bundled into the PS5 Deluxe.
- **No steelbook or physical goodies.** Those live in the Amazon-exclusive SteelBook Edition ($69.99, physical, ships October 15) — a separate purchase entirely.
- **No "better" version of the game.** Performance, features, and content are identical across editions.

<CommonMistake title="The most common deluxe mistake">
Don't assume "Deluxe = early access" on every platform. The 48-hour early access exists **only** in the PS5 Digital Deluxe Edition. Buying Deluxe on Xbox or PC expecting early play will disappoint you — everyone on those platforms unlocks September 24.
</CommonMistake>

## FAQ

### Is the Control Resonant Deluxe Edition worth it on PS5?
Yes, if you're buying at launch — the 48-hour early access (September 22) is exclusive to the PS5 Digital Deluxe and is the main reason to spend the extra $10. You also get the AWE Mission Outfit, Untapped Artifact, Starter Resource Bundle, artbook, and soundtrack.

### Is the Deluxe worth it on Xbox or PC?
Only if you want the cosmetics and digital extras. There's no early access on Xbox or PC — every edition unlocks September 24 — so the $10 buys the outfit, artifact, resource bundle, artbook, and soundtrack. If those don't matter to you, Standard is the smarter buy.

### What's included in the Deluxe Edition?
The full game plus: AWE Mission Outfit, Untapped Artifact (Wallet), Starter Resource Bundle, digital artbook, original soundtrack, and (PS5 only) 48-hour early access. Pre-order bonuses (Hiss Corruption Outfit, Pickpocket's Tool Artifact, Occult Outfit on PS5) come with any digital edition.

### Does the Deluxe include early access on all platforms?
No — only the **PS5 Digital Deluxe Edition** includes 48-hour early access. Xbox and PC Deluxe buyers unlock September 24 like everyone else.

### Is there any exclusive story content in the Deluxe?
No. All editions contain the same game — the Deluxe's extras are cosmetic items, a small in-game resource/artifact bundle, the artbook, and the soundtrack.

### Will the Deluxe content be sold separately later?
Almost certainly. Remedy has sold cosmetic packs and artbooks separately after launch for previous titles. If you're patient, the AWE Outfit and soundtrack will likely be available on their own later.
