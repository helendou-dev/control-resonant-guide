The headline perk — **48-hour early access on September 22** — comes **only with the PS5 Digital Deluxe Edition**. On Xbox and PC, no edition gets early access. This guide breaks down exactly what each edition includes so you can decide before September 22.

## Deluxe Edition Bonuses, Explained

Here's what the extra $10 buys, and whether each item actually matters:

**👕 AWE Mission Outfit** — An alternate outfit for Dylan, styled after the FBC's Federal Bureau of Control tactical gear (AWE = Altered World Event). Purely cosmetic — this is the "I work for the Bureau" drip option.

**👛 Untapped Artifact (Wallet)** — A unique in-game artifact for Dylan. Gameplay utility isn't detailed yet, but artifact-type bonuses in *Control* traditionally map to passive perks — expect a small early-game benefit rather than anything build-defining.

**🎒 Starter Resource Bundle** — A pack of early-game resources (materials, consumables, or upgrade currency — exact contents unconfirmed). It accelerates your first few hours of [weapon mastery](/games/control-resonant/combat-systems-preview), not a pay-to-win lever.

**📖 Digital artbook & soundtrack** — The artbook covers the game's "warped Manhattan" concept work, and the soundtrack runs ~40 tracks. For Remedy fans who already own the *Control* artbook, this slots right next to it.

**⚡ 48-hour early access (PS5 only)** — The only gameplay-relevant Deluxe perk, and it's PlayStation-exclusive. On PS5, Deluxe buyers start playing September 22; everyone else waits until September 24.

## The 48-Hour PS5 Early Access, in Practical Terms

The early access is the headline pre-order perk, so let's be precise about what it means:

- **It's tied to the PS5 Digital Deluxe Edition only** — the $69.99 Deluxe on PlayStation. PS5 Standard ($59.99) unlocks September 24.
- **Xbox and PC Deluxe buyers do NOT get early access** — the 48-hour window is a PS5-exclusive perk, not a Deluxe-exclusive perk.
- The **worldwide launch** is September 24 on PS5, Xbox, and PC.

Why does it matter? Three reasons: you get two extra days before the internet floods with guides, you avoid day-one queue issues, and if the game has a strong first-weekend meta, you're two days ahead of it. If you're on PS5 and you know you're buying the game anyway, the Deluxe's early window is the strongest single reason to pay the $10 premium.

</InfoBox>

## FAQ

### Does the Standard edition include early access?
No. On PS5, only the **Digital Deluxe Edition** ($69.99) includes the 48-hour early access window (September 22). The Standard edition ($59.99) unlocks September 24.

### What's the difference between Standard and Deluxe?
The Deluxe edition adds the AWE Mission Outfit, Untapped Artifact (Wallet), Starter Resource Bundle, digital artbook + soundtrack, and — on PS5 only — 48-hour early access. The game itself is identical.

### When does early access start?
September 22, 2026 — but only for the PS5 Digital Deluxe Edition. The worldwide launch follows on September 24, 2026 for everyone else.

### Can I get early access on Xbox or PC?
No. The 48-hour early access is exclusive to the PlayStation 5 Digital Deluxe Edition.

### What's the Steelbook Edition?
A $69.99 physical edition (PS5/Xbox) exclusive to Amazon: the base game disc, a Steelbook case, three fine-art prints, and a key-art poster. It ships October 15 — no early access, and digital pre-order bonuses aren't confirmed with it.

### Will Deluxe cosmetics be sold separately later?
Almost certainly — cosmetic packs and artbooks from Remedy's previous titles have been made available separately after launch. If you're patient, the Deluxe's extras will likely be purchasable on their own.

### Is Control Resonant pre-order available on Mac?
The Mac version of the game arrives later in 2026, and pre-orders for it are not live yet.

## The Verdict

**On PS5, the Deluxe is the play-early pick** — its 48-hour early access (September 22) is the only way to move your launch weekend forward, and it bundles cosmetics, the artbook, soundtrack, and a resource pack for the same $10. If you're buying on PlayStation anyway and want day-one play, Deluxe is the clear choice. **On Xbox and PC, pre-ordering is a neutral act** — no early access exists for you, so you're not losing anything by waiting for day-one impressions, and the Deluxe's $10 of cosmetics is the only thing tempting you early. If you do go Deluxe, know exactly what you're paying for: a head start on PS5, or skins and extras on Xbox/PC — nothing that changes whether the game is worth it.
