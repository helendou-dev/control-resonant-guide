Control Resonant ships in **four editions** across digital and physical: Standard ($59.99), Digital Deluxe ($69.99), a physical Standard disc ($59.99), and the Amazon-exclusive SteelBook ($69.99). Two editions, a $10 gap, and one platform-exclusive perk — the **48-hour early access, which comes only with the PS5 Digital Deluxe**.

This page is the complete edition comparison: what's inside each version, what the $10 gets you, and which one to buy for your platform. All prices and contents verified against the PlayStation Store, Steam, Epic, and Remedy's official breakdown (updated August 27, 2026).

## Editions at a Glance

| Edition | Price | Format | Release | Early access |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Standard** | $59.99 | Digital | September 24 | ❌ |
| **Digital Deluxe** | $69.99 | Digital | September 24 (PS5: Sept 22) | ✅ PS5 only |
| **Standard (Physical)** | $59.99 | Disc | October 15 | ❌ |
| **SteelBook** | $69.99 | Disc + extras | October 15 | ❌ |

Every digital edition includes the pre-order bonuses (Hiss Corruption Outfit + Pickpocket's Tool Artifact, plus the Occult Outfit on PS5). Physical editions are not listed with the digital pre-order bonuses — confirm with your retailer if that matters to you.

## Standard Edition — $59.99 (Digital)

The base game, available from **September 24** on PS5, Xbox Series X|S, PC (Steam & Epic Games Store).

- The full game — Dylan Faden's warped-Manhattan campaign, ~30 hours main story.
- Digital pre-orders include the **Hiss Corruption Outfit** and **Pickpocket's Tool Artifact**.
- **PS5 Standard does NOT include the 48-hour early access** — that's Deluxe-only. PS5 Standard unlocks September 24 like everyone else.

If you just want the game, this is the entry point. There is no "Standard + early access" upgrade path on PS5 — the early window only exists inside the Deluxe.

## Digital Deluxe Edition — $69.99 (Digital)

The full game plus five digital extras — and, on PS5, the **48-hour early access** (play from September 22):

| Deluxe content | What it is |
|---|---|
| **48-hour early access** | PS5 only — play September 22 instead of 24 |
| **AWE Mission Outfit** | Alternate Dylan outfit, FBC tactical style |
| **Untapped Artifact (Wallet)** | Unique in-game artifact for Dylan |
| **Starter Resource Bundle** | Early-game materials / consumables pack |
| **Digital artbook** | "Warped Manhattan" concept art |
| **Original soundtrack** | ~40 tracks |

The $10 premium on **PS5** buys early access *plus* all the extras — the strongest value case of any edition. On **Xbox and PC**, the same $10 buys only the cosmetics, artbook, soundtrack, and resource pack — no early access.

## Standard (Physical) — $59.99 (Disc)

The disc version for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, delayed to **October 15, 2026** alongside the SteelBook.

- Ships three weeks after the digital launch.
- Not listed with the digital pre-order bonuses on Remedy's official site.
- No early access, obviously — discs don't unlock early.

## SteelBook Edition — $69.99 (Amazon Exclusive)

The collector's pick: the base game disc in a red-and-black Steelbook case, plus three fine-art prints and a key-art poster. PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, $69.99, exclusive to Amazon, shipping October 15.

- The $10 over Standard physical buys the Steelbook case, prints, and poster — pure collector value, zero gameplay value.
- Same caveats as the Standard disc: no early access, digital bonuses not confirmed.

## The PS5 Early Access Question

The single most confusing part of Control Resonant's edition lineup — let's settle it:

- **48-hour early access (September 22) = PS5 Digital Deluxe only.**
- PS5 Standard: no early access, unlocks September 24.
- Xbox and PC (any edition): no early access, unlock September 24.

If playing two days early matters to you, there is exactly one path: the **$69.99 PS5 Digital Deluxe**. No other edition, platform, or store grants early play.

## FAQ

### How many editions of Control Resonant are there?
Four: Standard (digital, $59.99), Digital Deluxe (digital, $69.99), Standard physical disc ($59.99), and the Amazon-exclusive SteelBook Edition ($69.99, PS5/Xbox).

### What's the difference between Deluxe and Standard?
The Deluxe ($69.99) adds the AWE Mission Outfit, Untapped Artifact (Wallet), Starter Resource Bundle, digital artbook, and soundtrack — plus the 48-hour early access **on PS5 only**. The game itself is identical.

### Is the SteelBook Edition worth it?
If you collect physical editions, yes — the Steelbook case, three art prints, and key-art poster justify the $10 over the Standard disc. If you just want to play, no: it ships October 15 with no early access, and the digital bonuses aren't confirmed with it.

### Does the PS5 Standard edition have early access?
No. The 48-hour early access (September 22) is exclusive to the PS5 **Digital Deluxe Edition**. PS5 Standard unlocks September 24.

### Is there a physical Deluxe edition?
No. The physical lineup is Standard disc and SteelBook only — the Deluxe's extras (AWE Outfit, artbook, etc.) exist only in the digital edition.

### Which store is cheapest for the digital editions?
All stores list Standard at $59.99 and Deluxe at $69.99 at launch (regional pricing varies). See the [pre-order guide](/games/control-resonant/preorder-guide) for the store-by-store breakdown and where the PS5 early access is available.

### Will the Deluxe extras be sold separately later?
Almost certainly — Remedy has made cosmetic packs and artbooks available separately after launch in the past. If you're patient, you can likely buy the AWE Outfit or soundtrack on their own later.
