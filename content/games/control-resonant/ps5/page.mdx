## The Headline: 48-Hour Early Access

The PS5 version's killer feature is simple: **pre-order the Digital Deluxe Edition on PS5 and you play on September 22, 2026 — two full days before the worldwide launch.**

What you need to know:

- **It's on the Deluxe only.** The 48-hour window comes with the PS5 Digital Deluxe Edition ($69.99). The Standard edition ($59.99) unlocks on September 24 like every other platform. On PS5, the Deluxe's extra $10 buys early access *plus* cosmetics and digital extras.
- **It's PS5-exclusive.** Xbox Series X|S and PC players get the game on the 24th with no early window — even if they buy Deluxe.
- **Why it matters:** two extra days before guides flood the internet, no day-one queue risk, and a head start on the launch-weekend [Aberrant meta](/games/control-resonant/combat-systems-preview).

## DualSense: The Aberrant in Your Hands

Remedy has confirmed the PS5 version leans hard into the DualSense:

- **Adaptive triggers communicate weapon forms.** The [Aberrant's five forms](/games/control-resonant/aberrant-weapon-forms) — Dual Blades, Axe, Scythe, Whip, Heavy Hammer — reportedly each carry distinct trigger resistance, so the controller tells you which form you're holding without looking at the UI.
- **Haptic feedback for hits and the Gap system.** Expect the DualSense to sell the weight of an Axe charged swing and the *break* moment when an enemy's [Gap meter](/games/control-resonant/combat-systems-preview) shatters.
- **The most immersive version on paper.** Alongside the 3D-audio possibilities of a warped Manhattan, the PS5 version is shaping up as the sensory flagship — assuming you play with a DualSense in hand.

<ProTip title="If you're on PC">
The PC version supports whatever controller you plug in, but the Aberrant trigger-resistance feedback is only fully implemented on the DualSense — and even on PC, adaptive trigger support in third-party PC ports is hit-or-miss unless Remedy explicitly enables it. If trigger feedback matters to you, that's a PS5 argument.
</ProTip>

## Performance & PS5 Pro Status

**Update (September 2026, via Wccftech gamescom interview with senior level designer Arhi Makkonen):** both the 60 FPS performance mode and the PS5 Pro enhancement are now confirmed.

- **60 FPS performance mode — confirmed.** The PS5 version uses the standard "classic setup": a Quality mode and a Performance mode, with Performance targeting 60 FPS. You choose in-game which one you want.
- **PS5 Pro enhancement — confirmed.** Remedy is working on the PS5 Pro version; Makkonen said it "should be" supported and that "it plays well," though final figures weren't available at gamescom. We'll update the page when the official Pro specs land.
- **Ray tracing:** the "warped Manhattan" marketing material suggests heavy ray-traced lighting — expect the Quality mode to prioritize RT effects at a lower frame rate, with Performance trading some lighting fidelity for 60 FPS.
- **PS5 only, not PS4.** No last-gen version exists or is planned.

## Storage & Pre-Load

Both confirmed as of September 8 (via PlayStation Game Size scraping the PlayStation Store):

- **Install size: 89.27 GB** (version 01.200.000). For reference, the Xbox build is **85.97 GB** and the PC build is **106.08 GB** — the PS5 download sits cleanly between the two, as expected from a comparable Northlight console build. Clear space now — day-one patches are inevitable, so budget ~100 GB on your SSD to be safe.
- **PS5 Standard pre-load: opens September 22, 2026** (the same day as the Deluxe early access). The PlayStation Store handles this automatically if you've pre-ordered and have **Settings → System → Automatic Downloads** enabled.
- **PS5 Deluxe pre-load: opens September 20, 2026** — two days ahead of Standard, so Deluxe buyers can have the game fully installed before the 48-hour early access unlocks on September 22.
- **Global launch unlock: 2 PM GMT on September 24, 2026** — that's 10 AM ET / 7 AM PT. The PS5 Deluxe 48-hour early access opens at the same 2 PM GMT on September 22.
- **Physical vs digital:** the physical PS5 edition was delayed to **October 15, 2026** — three weeks after digital launch. The 48-hour early window comes with the PS5 Digital Deluxe pre-order on the PlayStation Store, so if early access matters to you, that digital edition is the only way to get it.
