If you're counting down the days to launch, here's everything the trailer showed, what it confirms about gameplay, and how it fits into the final month before release.

## What the "Altered State" Trailer Actually Shows

The trailer frames its story simply: **Manhattan is overrun, and Dylan Faden is humanity's last hope.** That's the narrow story window Remedy chose to reveal — but the footage packs in a lot of gameplay detail.

According to Remedy and PlayStation's official descriptions, the trailer highlights four pillars: **fluid movement, stylish combat, powerful executions, and unpredictable encounters.** If you've been following our [combat systems preview](/games/control-resonant/combat-systems-preview), you'll recognize all of them — but the trailer shows them faster and more aggressively than any previous footage:

- **Airborne movement everywhere.** Earlier hands-on coverage described Dylan's toolkit as two air dashes, a double jump, and brief levitation. The trailer leans hard into that verticality — Dylan spends as much time airborne as on the ground.
- **The Aberrant shifting forms mid-combat.** The [shape-shifting weapon](/games/control-resonant/aberrant-weapon-forms) morphs between configurations as Dylan strings combos together.
- **Executions as the centerpiece.** Melee hits restore the Combat Ability resource, abilities stun enemies, and stunned enemies are open to executions — which temporarily boost melee damage. The trailer's execution shots are its most brutal moments.
- **Reality-bending environments.** Altered architecture and paranatural distortions consistent with the Remedy Connected Universe — the DNA Control (2019) and Alan Wake 2 share.

For a deeper breakdown of how these systems work together — and how they differ from Jesse's ranged-heavy kit in the original Control — see our full [combat systems preview](/games/control-resonant/combat-systems-preview).

## The Trailer Didn't Change Any Release Plans

One thing the trailer *didn't* do: move any dates. The September 24, 2026 worldwide digital launch on PS5, Xbox Series X|S, Steam, and Epic Games Store was reaffirmed, not revised.

That matters because the [gone gold announcement](/games/control-resonant/gone-gold) on August 18 locked the launch build — and this trailer doubles as the start of Remedy's final marketing push. If you're planning your launch week, our [release date guide](/games/control-resonant/release-date) has the full schedule, including the PS5 Digital Deluxe 48-hour early access window starting September 22.

## The First (and Only) Public Hands-On Demo

Alongside the trailer, Remedy confirmed something bigger: **a playable Control Resonant demo at gamescom itself**, at the Level Infinite booth in Hall 6, from **August 26 through August 30, 2026**.

This is the only officially confirmed pre-launch hands-on opportunity. No home demo, no beta, no playtest — the gamescom booth is it. For the full picture on demo and beta availability (and why you shouldn't trust any "beta signup" page you find online), see our [demo, beta and playtest guide](/games/control-resonant/demo-beta-playtest).

## CONTROL Resonant x ZA/UM Atelier: Real-World Merch

The third announcement to land alongside the trailer: a **five-piece fashion collection from ZA/UM Atelier** (the studio behind Disco Elysium's art direction) inspired by the institutional wardrobe of the Federal Bureau of Control.

The collection emphasizes "utility, longevity, and thoughtful design," was on display at gamescom from August 26–30, and is available for pre-order now via ZA/UM Atelier. It's a made-to-order line, not a mass retail drop — expect premium pricing and longer fulfillment windows.

It's a small signal, but a telling one: Remedy is treating Control Resonant as a franchise-scale launch, with merch partnerships that Control (2019) never had.

## FAQ

### When did the Altered State trailer come out?

August 25, 2026, during gamescom Opening Night Live in Cologne. It was published online the same night.

### What song is in the Control Resonant trailer?

"Omen" by The Prodigy. Remedy has a history of licensing iconic tracks for trailers — Control (2019)'s launch marketing used a similar approach.

### Does the trailer show new story details?

The core story frame — Manhattan overrun, Dylan as humanity's last hope — is confirmed. Beyond that, Remedy kept story specifics close to the chest. For everything narrative-related, see our [story recap](/games/control-resonant/story-recap).

### Will there be another trailer before launch?

Nothing is confirmed. Remedy hasn't announced additional pre-launch milestones between gamescom and September 24, so "Altered State" may be the final trailer.

### Can I still play the gamescom demo?

The gamescom demo ran August 26–30 at the Level Infinite booth in Hall 6 — it requires a gamescom ticket and ended when the show closed. For everyone else, the earliest way to play is the PS5 Digital Deluxe Edition's [48-hour early access](/games/control-resonant/deluxe-edition-worth-it) starting September 22.

### Is the ZA/UM collection limited?

It's a made-to-order collection, so there's no hard cap announced — but made-to-order runs typically have a fulfillment window. If you want a piece, ordering during the pre-order period is the safe play.

## Bottom Line

The "Altered State" trailer is the clearest signal yet that Control Resonant's combat identity is **speed** — Dylan is an airborne, execution-hungry brawler, a deliberate contrast to Jesse's methodical gun-and-telekinesis kit. Combined with the gamescom demo, the ZA/UM collection, and top-3 preorder rankings on PS5, the final month of buildup is officially underway. See you on September 22 — or September 24, depending on your edition.
