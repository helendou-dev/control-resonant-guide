## Why the Timing Itself Is Good News

Six days between reviews and launch is on the generous end for a single-player AAA. The comparison worth making:

- Publishers confident in quality lift embargoes **4–7 days out** — they want positive word-of-mouth compounding into launch week.
- Publishers nervous about reception hold reviews to **24–48 hours before launch** (or launch day itself) — enough time for headlines, not enough for deliberation.

Remedy choosing September 18 for a September 24 ship — with the announcement made a full three days before the lift — reads like a studio that knows what the scores say. It's the same confidence signal as the CEO's public "must-have day-one purchase" framing and the 1.5M+ wishlist count. Of course, confidence and quality aren't the same thing: the reviews themselves settle that, starting today.

With hindsight, the September 18 lift is closest to the original *Control*'s pattern — not the tight *Alan Wake 2* leash. For a sequel banking on Dylan Faden's story, that told us Remedy wasn't afraid of story spoilers driving the conversation; they wanted the scores working for them all week.

## What Reviews Will Tell You (and What They Won't)

Reviews are most useful for three day-one questions:

1. **Performance**: Which platform runs best? Is the PC version optimized, or does it need patches? (This is often the deciding factor for PC buyers.)
2. **Combat depth**: Does the shape-shifting Aberrant combat live up to the *DOOM (2016)* / *Sekiro* inspirations Remedy has cited? Early previews were positive but limited to short demos.
3. **Content volume**: Main story length (~30 hours per our [playtime guide](/games/control-resonant/how-long-to-beat)), side content quality, and whether the open-world Manhattan structure holds up.

What reviews **won't** tell you: your own preference. Difficulty is [fully adjustable](/games/control-resonant/difficulty-settings), so a reviewer who dislikes the challenge might not reflect your experience — check whether the reviewer played with assists or specific settings.
