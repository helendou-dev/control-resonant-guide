Preload info appears in the same place you bought the game:

If you already pre-ordered and have **auto-download enabled**, your console or launcher handles it automatically — you'll get a notification, not a chore.

## How Preload Actually Works

Preload downloads the encrypted game files ahead of launch. What you can and can't do:

- ✅ **Download everything before launch** — most of the install happens on preload day, so launch-day load is trivial;
- ✅ **Preload the day-one patch?** No — patches ship at unlock, but they're small relative to the base install;
- ❌ **Play before the unlock time** — preloaded files are encrypted and stay locked until the release timestamp. Your clock-watching starts at unlock, not download.

For PS5 early-access players this means: preload Sept 19–20 (expected), then at the September 22 unlock the encryption lifts and you're in — no waiting around for a 90 GB download on day one.
