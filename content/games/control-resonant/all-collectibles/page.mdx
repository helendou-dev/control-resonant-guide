**Short answer: Control Resonant has 67 collectibles across 8 types — 7 FBC Taskboards, 11 Dylan's Sketchbooks, 8 Dogs, 6 Secret Hideouts, 6 Testing Facilities, 7 Taxis, 14 Freeze Frame Cameras, and 8 Pope's Research Balloons. Nothing is missable: every single one can be collected after the story in free roam, and that's genuinely the best time to do it, because a full traversal kit removes every barrier in one pass.**

## The Complete Checklist: 8 Types, 67 Items

| # | Collectible Type | Count | What It Is | Reward |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **FBC Taskboards** | 7 | District notice boards that hand out optional FBC Tasks — complete any **20 of 42** tasks total | *Diligent Consultant* achievement |
| 2 | **Dylan's Sketchbooks** | 11 | Dylan's own sketchbooks, each hidden behind a small object-arrangement puzzle | *Work-Life Balance* achievement |
| 3 | **Dogs** | 8 | Very good dogs, scattered across the districts — each one is a pet-and-done interaction | *Every Dog Has Her Day* achievement |
| 4 | **Secret Hideouts** | 6 | Unmarked backroom basements behind environmental puzzles, each hiding a permanent Health Upgrade | *Backroom Espionage* achievement |
| 5 | **Testing Facilities** | 6 | Threshold research sites tied to the testing side quest chain | One requirement toward *Threshold Researcher* |
| 6 | **Taxis** | 7 | Destroyed cabs near fast travel points; each one's lights lead into a Threshold, and each completed stop unlocks one of seven campfire stories | One requirement toward *Threshold Researcher* |
| 7 | **Freeze Frame Cameras** | 14 | Hidden cameras to find and interact with across all districts | One requirement toward *Panopticon Intern* |
| 8 | **Pope's Research Balloons** | 8 | Dr. Pope's escaped research balloons, tied to the Pope's Research side quest | *Budding Analyst* achievement |

**67:7+11+8+6+6+7+14+8.** The in-game tracker lives in the pause menu's Case Files tab, but it only shows a **percentage per district** — not a per-item checklist. If you're going for all 67, keep your own count.

Two structural notes worth knowing before you start:

- **Seven of the eight types are tied to a side quest** — start the associated quest and the game starts leading you toward that type's items. Secret Hideouts are the lone exception: no quest, no marker, no hints beyond a question mark that fades in when you're practically standing on them. We have the [full hideout walkthrough](/games/control-resonant/secret-hideouts) with every puzzle solution.
- **The counts above are fixed.** None of the 67 spawn randomly or shift between playthroughs.

## Nothing Is Missable — Really

The single most common collectibles question for any Remedy game is "what did I permanently lock out?" — and for once, the answer is **nothing**.

- **All 67 collectibles remain accessible after the credits.** Free roam continues post-story, and no collectible is behind a point of no return.
- **No collectible is difficulty-locked or platform-locked.** The locations and requirements are identical on every difficulty, including [Assist Mode](/games/control-resonant/difficulty-settings) settings.
- **The only truly missable thing in the entire game isn't a collectible** — it's the *Be What You Want to Be* achievement (picking the correct karaoke lyrics in *The Party Won't Stop* side quest), and even that can be recovered by reloading the auto-save before the sequence ends.

The catch runs the other direction: on a first visit to most districts you will be **missing traversal abilities** needed to actually reach a chunk of the collectibles. Which brings us to the gates.

## The Ability Gates: Why Post-Story Collection Wins

Four traversal unlocks wall off most of the 67. All of them come from the **"Search for Jesse" main quest line** and from **defeating each zone's Resonant** — i.e., story progression you can't skip:

| Unlock Source | Ability | What It Opens |
|---|---|---|
| Jesse Sightings: The Incursion Fault | **Shift** (wall-running) | Vertical surfaces — rooftop and upper-floor collectibles across every district |
| Jesse Sightings: The Subway Fault | **Reach** (grappling) | Grapple points — gaps and ledges that are simply unreachable without it |
| Defeat the West Incursion Zone Resonant | **Ground Slam** | **Ability Barriers** — the glowing floors/walls sealing off several Testing Facilities and other interiors |
| Defeat the Central Zone Resonant | **Ignite / Inferno** (flame) | The web-blocked passages scattered through later districts |

On top of those: **fully exploring the final region, The Unknown, requires completing the Pope's Research side quest** — the same quest the 8 Balloons belong to. Two birds, one quest.

This is why the fastest route for a completionist looks like:

1. **Play the story through to the credits**, picking up whatever you naturally pass (the 8 dogs and most Taskboards basically collect themselves this way).
2. **Finish Pope's Research** before heading into The Unknown.
3. **Sweep the map region by region** with the full ability kit — nothing can wall you anymore.

## The Route: By Region, Not by Type

Control Resonant's fast travel only works through **Fast Travel Doors** — 32 of them across the map, and you have to physically walk to a door every single time. There's no map-screen teleport. That one design decision dictates the entire optimal route:

> **Collect by region, not by type.** Hunting all 11 Sketchbooks across seven districts means crossing the map seven times. Clearing one district's full list — every type — before moving to the next means you cross it once.

The seven regions, in the order most players find smoothest for a post-story sweep:

1. **Downtown**
2. **Central**
3. **West Incursion Zone**
4. **Evacuation Zone**
5. **The Park**
6. **Underpass**
7. **The Unknown** (do Pope's Research first)

Within each region, hit the fast travel door first and work outward from it. Most of the 7 Taxis are parked **near fast travel points** by design — the game routes you past them.

## The Upgrade Economy: Crates, Hideouts, and Your 12+12

Collectibles and permanent upgrades overlap in one place: **Large Resource Crates**, which contain Health Upgrades and Power Upgrades. You need **any 12 of each** for the character-building baseline, and there are well more than 12 in the world.

- **The 6 Secret Hideouts and 6 Testing Facilities each contain upgrades** — that's 6 Health + 6 Power (plus what the hideouts hand you) before you've opened a single random crate.
- You only need roughly **6 more of each from the world at large**, and those are hard to miss on a full region sweep.
- Translation: if you're doing the 67-collectible run, your 12+12 upgrades come along for free. See [why the Health Upgrades matter](/games/control-resonant/secret-hideouts) for the survivability math against the tougher Resonants.

## How This Fits a Platinum Run

The 67 collectibles sit inside a 50-trophy list (1 Platinum / 1 Gold / 16 Silver / 32 Bronze) that's more time investment than skill test. The collectible-adjacent trophies are all covered by this checklist:

- *Diligent Consultant* (20 of 42 FBC Tasks), *Work-Life Balance* (11 Sketchbooks), *Every Dog Has Her Day* (8 Dogs), *Backroom Espionage* (6 Hideouts), *Threshold Researcher* (Testing Facilities + Taxis), *Panopticon Intern* (includes the 14 Cameras), *Budding Analyst* (8 Balloons).

The genuinely grindy trophies live elsewhere — the 30-Artifact crafting chain is the biggest time sink — and the zone-leveling trophies want you to level every Zone to 5 as you go rather than backtracking late. If you're building toward the Platinum, treat this page as the map half of the plan and the [skill tree](/games/control-resonant/skill-tree) as the build half.

One NG+ note: collectibles are a fresh checklist per cycle, and NG++ [resets zone secrets and world events](/games/control-resonant/new-game-plus) on the same save — completionists get a second (and third) pass at all of it with a maxed kit.

## FAQ

**How many collectibles are in Control Resonant?**
67 across 8 types: 7 FBC Taskboards, 11 Dylan's Sketchbooks, 8 Dogs, 6 Secret Hideouts, 6 Testing Facilities, 7 Taxis, 14 Freeze Frame Cameras, and 8 Pope's Research Balloons. Fast Travel Doors (32) are not collectibles and don't count.

**Are any collectibles missable?**
No. All 67 remain collectible after the story in free roam, on any difficulty. The game's only true missable is the karaoke achievement, and even that's fixable by reloading the auto-save.

**What's the best time to collect everything?**
After the credits, with all four traversal abilities unlocked and Pope's Research completed for The Unknown. Collecting mid-story is fine for whatever you naturally pass, but you'll hit ability walls constantly.

**Do I need to complete all 42 FBC Tasks?**
No — 20 of 42 unlocks *Diligent Consultant*. The Taskboards themselves (7) are the collectible; the tasks are a completion step you can pick and choose from.

**What do the Secret Hideouts give you?**
A permanent Health Upgrade in every basement (6 total), plus intel — and they're the only collectible type with no side quest pointing at them. Full puzzle solutions in the [Secret Hideouts guide](/games/control-resonant/secret-hideouts), including the Urban Fresh Mart **1213** code and the café **BDAC** answer.

**What are the Taxis about?**
Each of the 7 taxis is found near a fast travel point. Its lights lead you into a Threshold, where you find the specific object it asks for. Each completed taxi unlocks one of seven campfire stories — seven friends, one per cab — and the full set completes part of the *Threshold Researcher* requirement alongside the 6 Testing Facilities.

**Can I use Assist Mode while collecting?**
Yes — Assist Mode doesn't disable any achievement, including everything on this list. Turn on whatever toggles you want and collect in peace.

**Do collectibles carry into New Game+?**
Your first playthrough's checklist is done — NG+ starts a fresh collectible checklist, so a second run gets its own 67. Nothing carries over half-finished.
