## Which Platforms Does Control Resonant Release On?

Control Resonant is a **current-gen and PC title only** — there is no PS4, Xbox One, or Nintendo Switch version announced, and don't expect one. It's built on Remedy's Northlight engine, which is doing a lot of heavy lifting for the game's "warped Manhattan" density, and that ambition is squarely aimed at the current hardware generation.

### PlayStation 5

The PS5 version is the flagship platform, and it comes with the headline pre-order perk: **48-hour early access**. PS5 players who pre-order any edition get in on **September 22, 2026** instead of the 24th.

Remedy has also confirmed the PS5 version makes use of the DualSense's haptic feedback and adaptive triggers — the shape-shifting Aberrant weapon reportedly uses trigger resistance to communicate which of the five [weapon forms](/games/control-resonant/aberrant-weapon-forms) you're currently wielding.

### Xbox Series X|S

Xbox Series X|S players get the game on September 24, 2026 with Smart Delivery, meaning your purchase covers both consoles in the family. As of now, **no early access has been announced for Xbox** — the 48-hour window is a PS5-exclusive pre-order perk. Game Pass has not been announced for Control Resonant at this time.

### PC (Steam & Epic Games Store)

PC players get the game on **both Steam and the Epic Games Store** simultaneously on September 24, 2026. Full cross-platform cloud saves between the two stores have not been confirmed yet — we'll update this page as soon as Remedy clarifies.

### Mac

The Mac version arrives **later in 2026** — a date has not been announced. Remedy has been building out native Apple Silicon support across its recent releases, and Control Resonant appears to follow the same path. If you're a Mac-only player, the delay also gives you time to let the day-one patches settle.
