## The Headline: 48-Hour Early Access

The PS5 version's killer feature is simple: **pre-order the Digital Deluxe Edition on PS5 and you play on September 22, 2026 — two full days before the worldwide launch.**

What you need to know:

- **It's on the Deluxe only.** The 48-hour window comes with the PS5 Digital Deluxe Edition ($69.99). The Standard edition ($59.99) unlocks on September 24 like every other platform. On PS5, the Deluxe's extra $10 buys early access *plus* cosmetics and digital extras.
- **It's PS5-exclusive.** Xbox Series X|S and PC players get the game on the 24th with no early window — even if they buy Deluxe.
- **Why it matters:** two extra days before guides flood the internet, no day-one queue risk, and a head start on the launch-weekend [Aberrant meta](/games/control-resonant/combat-systems-preview).

## DualSense: The Aberrant in Your Hands

Remedy has confirmed the PS5 version leans hard into the DualSense:

- **Adaptive triggers communicate weapon forms.** The [Aberrant's five forms](/games/control-resonant/aberrant-weapon-forms) — Dual Blades, Axe, Scythe, Whip, Heavy Hammer — reportedly each carry distinct trigger resistance, so the controller tells you which form you're holding without looking at the UI.
- **Haptic feedback for hits and the Gap system.** Expect the DualSense to sell the weight of an Axe charged swing and the *break* moment when an enemy's [Gap meter](/games/control-resonant/combat-systems-preview) shatters.
- **The most immersive version on paper.** Alongside the 3D-audio possibilities of a warped Manhattan, the PS5 version is shaping up as the sensory flagship — assuming you play with a DualSense in hand.

<ProTip title="If you're on PC">
The PC version supports whatever controller you plug in, but the Aberrant trigger-resistance feedback is only fully implemented on the DualSense — and even on PC, adaptive trigger support in third-party PC ports is hit-or-miss unless Remedy explicitly enables it. If trigger feedback matters to you, that's a PS5 argument.

</ProTip>
