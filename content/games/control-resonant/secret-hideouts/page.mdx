**Short answer: Control Resonant has six secret hideouts — one per district except the Underpass — and none of them are marked on your map until you're practically standing on them. Each one is locked behind an environmental puzzle, and each basement hides a permanent Health Upgrade. Clear all six and you unlock a bonus achievement on top.**

## How Secret Hideouts Work

Backroom hideouts are Control Resonant's love letter to players who check every suspicious storefront. The rules:

- **They never appear on the map on their own.** Walk within a certain distance of a hideout and a question mark marker fades in — that's your only hint one is nearby.
- **Every hideout is a puzzle first, a room second.** Each storefront front hides an environmental puzzle; solving it opens a hidden door or staircase down to the basement.
- **Every basement contains a permanent Health Upgrade.** Six hideouts, six upgrades — that's the bulk of your max-health pool sitting behind puzzles, not combat.
- **Clear all six and a bonus achievement unlocks** — the game's reward for treating backrooms like a proper checklist rather than hoping you stumble through them all.

The six hideouts are spread across **Downtown, Central, the Evacuation Zone, the West Incursion Zone, the Park, and the Unknown**. The Underpass is the only district without one. You can do them in any order, but the Downtown hideout needs late-game traversal (see below), so most players finish that one last.

## All Six Hideouts at a Glance

| # | District | Location | Puzzle | Solution |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Downtown | Atlantis Café (north of the Oldest House) | Activate six appliances in the correct order | Smallest to largest — see below |
| 2 | Central | Laundromat (east of the Field Office) | Set seven rows of washing machines to match a 0/1 clue | Five on, the rest off — pattern below |
| 3 | Evacuation Zone | Consting Gallery (near the center, beside a fast travel point) | Light the four paintings described as sold | One painting per wall group |
| 4 | West Incursion Zone | Urban Fresh Mart (south of the fast travel point) | Four-digit register code, hinted by flashing soda-machine lights | **1213** |
| 5 | Park | Atlantis Café (southeast, near the research station) | Match table orders to menu letters | **BDAC** |
| 6 | Unknown | Pulse Fitness gym (middle of the district, lower level) | Operate gym equipment in a workout order | Treadmill/bike sequence below |

## Hideout 1: Downtown — Atlantis Café

The first Atlantis Café sits north of the Oldest House — the catch is that you have to cross past an electronic road sign to reach it, and getting inside requires **Gravity Shift**. That's a late-game traversal ability, so this is the one hideout most players naturally finish last. Don't burn an hour on it early; come back when your movement kit is complete.

Once inside:

1. **Pull the wall-mounted electrical switch** — this powers the puzzle.
2. **Activate the appliances in order from smallest to largest.** The game doesn't tell you the rule; you learn it by failing. The order:

| Order | Appliance | Where |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Radio | Opposite wall, dining-table side |
| 2 | Coffee machine | Counter |
| 3 | White coffee machine | Counter |
| 4 | Soda machine | Against the back wall |
| 5 | Microwave | Kitchen area |
| 6 | Refrigerator | Largest unit in the room |

Get one wrong and the sequence resets from the top — annoying, but harmless. The hidden door opens once all six run in the correct order. Take the stairs down and claim the Health Upgrade.

## Hideout 2: Central — The Laundromat

East of the Central Field Office. The entrance is blocked by red light and garbage, and the way in is the puzzle's first step: **three red streaks fly toward the door — trace them back to their source and destroy it.** The door unjams itself.

Inside, the wall holds a clue of seven rows of zeroes and ones — and the laundromat has seven rows of washing machines. **1 means on, 0 means off.** Standing in front of the machines, left to right, per row:

| Row | Setting |
|---|---|
| 1 | Only the middle machine on |
| 2 | The middle two machines on |
| 3 | First and last machines on |
| 4 | All off |
| 5 | All off |
| 6 | All off |
| 7 | Only the leftmost machine on |

Watch out for rows 4–6: machines that are already lit need to be **manually switched off** — the clue assumes you start from scratch, the room doesn't. Correct configuration opens the door to the basement and the Health Upgrade.

## Hideout 3: Evacuation Zone — Consting Gallery

The gallery sits near the center of the Evacuation Zone, right beside a fast travel point — the easiest hideout to reach, and a good first stop if you're routing them.

The document on the counter lists the paintings that have been **sold**: *Journey*, *Window*, *Thunder*, and *Tide*. Your job is to turn on the light above the one painting per wall group that matches each description:

| Wall group | Painting to light | How to recognize it |
|---|---|---|
| Left wall, first group | Middle painting | A path ending at a lone tree (*Journey*) |
| Front wall | Leftmost painting | A mass of twisted branches (*Thunder*) |
| Right-adjacent group | Leftmost painting | A lone V-shaped tree on open plain (*Window*) |
| Right wall, second group | Middle painting | A tree fallen into water, reflected (*Tide*) |

All four lit correctly and a hidden passage opens on the left side of the front wall. Health Upgrade waiting below.

## Hideout 4: West Incursion Zone — Urban Fresh Mart

From the West Incursion Zone fast travel point, head south until you hit the Urban Fresh Mart on the street. Inside, a register asks for a **four-digit code**.

The clue chain: the soda machine's lights flash in a sequence, and a note in the store tells you the flashing order needs to be **reversed** before you type it in. Follow the flashes, reverse them, and the register accepts:

> **Register code: 1213**

The hidden entrance opens beside the counter. Down the stairs: Health Upgrade number four.

## Hideout 5: Park — Atlantis Café

Yes, a second Atlantis Café — this one in the Park's southeast, near the research station. No appliances this time; this puzzle is a **logic match** between table numbers, customer orders, and the café menu.

The note gives you the table order; the flyers on those tables tell you the drinks; the menu maps each drink to a letter:

| Table | Drink | Letter |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Iced coffee | B |
| 5 | Spiced chai | D |
| 4 | Espresso | A |
| 2 | Caramel latte | C |

> **Register code: BDAC**

The hidden door beside the counter opens. Basement: Health Upgrade plus bonus intel.

## Hideout 6: Unknown — Pulse Fitness

The last hideout is inside Pulse Fitness, the gym in the middle of the Unknown district — the gym itself is on the **lower level**.

The locker room note lays out a workout order, and the puzzle is recreating it exactly on the interactive equipment:

| Step | Action |
|---|---|
| 1 | Set the treadmill to 1 km/h |
| 2 | Set the exercise bike to 24 km/h |
| 3 | Use the vending machine |
| 4 | Set the treadmill to 24 km/h |
| 5 | Set the exercise bike to 1 km/h |

Complete the sequence and the final hidden room reveals itself. Health Upgrade, and — if you've cleared the other five — the achievement for completing every backroom hideout.

## Why the Health Upgrades Matter

Six permanent max-health bumps is a meaningful chunk of survivability, and unlike most of Control Resonant's power curve, none of it comes from combat. If you're bouncing off a [Resonant boss](/games/control-resonant/bosses) — The Dancer's rhythm chains and The Collective's add-swarm phases both punish a small health pool — the hideouts are the fastest way to get tankier without touching [difficulty settings](/games/control-resonant/difficulty-settings).

A few routing notes:

- **Do the Evacuation Zone gallery early** — it's beside a fast travel point and takes two minutes.
- **Save Downtown's café for last** — the Gravity Shift requirement means it's structurally a late-game stop anyway.
- **The Park and West Incursion hideouts fit naturally into quest routes** — the Park's southeast research station and the West Incursion fast travel point both put you within marker range.
- **After the story, free roam continues** — but the health upgrades help most *during* the campaign, so don't defer the whole checklist to the endgame.
- **Planning an NG+ run?** The Mold power choice is the cycle's real lock (see the [NG+ guide](/games/control-resonant/new-game-plus)) — health upgrades are per-character progression, so grab them whenever.

## FAQ

**How many secret hideouts are in Control Resonant?**
Six — one each in Downtown, Central, the Evacuation Zone, the West Incursion Zone, the Park, and the Unknown. The Underpass has none.

**Do hideouts show on the map?**
Not until you're close. Approach within a certain range and a question mark marker appears — that's the only in-game hint. There's no quest, no dialogue pointer, and no checklist in the UI.

**What do I get for each hideout?**
A permanent Health Upgrade in every basement, plus intel items in some. Clearing all six unlocks a bonus achievement on top of the six upgrades.

**What are the hideout codes and answers?**
Urban Fresh Mart register: **1213** (the soda machine's flashing sequence, reversed). Park Atlantis Café register: **BDAC** (table 1 = B, table 5 = D, table 4 = A, table 2 = C). The other four hideouts are order/matching puzzles — full solutions in the sections above.

**Which abilities do I need?**
Only the Downtown Atlantis Café has a hard requirement — Gravity Shift to reach it. The rest are reachable with baseline traversal; some vertical approaches get easier with a fuller movement kit.

**Are the hideouts missable?**
Launch-week guides don't report any hideout locking out after story events, and free roam continues after the credits. The bigger risk is simply never finding them — they're unmarked.

**Do the puzzle solutions change?**
The codes and sequences are fixed in the retail build (day-one hotfix 1.3.3) — they're deterministic puzzles, not randomized ones. If a future patch alters any solution, we'll update this page.
