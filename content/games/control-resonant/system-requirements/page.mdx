## Why We Expect These Numbers

### The console baseline sets the floor

Control Resonant is **current-gen only** — no PS4 or Xbox One versions. That means the minimum PC spec has to approximate a PS5-class machine: modern 8-core Zen 2+ CPU, ~10 TFLOPs of GPU power, fast NVMe storage. A GTX 1660 Super with heavy upscaling is roughly where that lands on PC.

### Northlight is a known quantity

The Northlight engine powered both *Control* and *Alan Wake 2*. Two patterns carry over:

1. **Ray tracing is core to the look.** *Control* was a flagship RT showcase in 2019, and *Alan Wake 2* pushed path-traced reflections and lighting. Expect Control Resonant's "warped Manhattan" to lean hard on RT — and to punish GPUs without it.
2. **VRAM matters more than raw FPS.** Remedy's games have historically run fine on mid-range cards but stutter when textures exceed VRAM. Prioritize **8 GB+ of VRAM** over a slightly faster 6 GB card.

### Storage: SSD is not optional

The PS5's storage architecture is a hard requirement target. Expect **80 GB of SSD space** — and treat a SATA SSD as the floor, with NVMe strongly preferred for traversal through Manhattan's districts. A spinning hard drive is a non-starter; don't pre-order the PC version planning to run it from an HDD.

## Control Resonant on Steam Deck?

Too early to say, but the signals are mixed. Remedy's last two games have been heavy Steam Deck asks — *Alan Wake 2* remains one of the most demanding titles in any handheld's library. If Control Resonant ships with FSR 3 support and a genuinely scalable low preset, a 30 FPS Deck experience at 800p low is plausible. Don't count on Verified status at launch.

## Settings That Will Matter Most

Based on Northlight behavior in *Control* and *Alan Wake 2*, these are the settings expected to carry the most performance weight:

1. **Ray-traced reflections** — the single biggest cost, and central to the game's look
2. **Volumetric lighting** — heavy in indoor paranatural spaces
3. **Texture quality** — VRAM-limited; drop this first if you see stutter
4. **Global illumination quality** — big visual payoff, big GPU cost
5. **Upscaling quality mode** — DLSS/FSR Quality will likely be the intended way to play
